Charting help — cheat sheet

AI Tools for Nurses • Education only — not medical advice &#bull; Never paste patient data

Golden rule: never paste patient data

  • No names, DOB, MRNs, room numbers, dates of service, photos.
  • Use “patient A”, “this afternoon,” “post-op day 2.”
  • Write from your own de-identified bullets first.

Step 1 — Write your bullets

Jot down what you did/observed on paper or a private note.

Step 2 — Organize into a note format

Prompt: “Turn these de-identified nursing notes into a SOAP note (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan). Plain professional nursing language. No names or dates. [de-identified bullets only]”

Step 3 — Know the formats

Prompt: “Difference between SOAP, DAP, SOAPIE notes? One short made-up example each, no real patient details.”

Step 4 — Tighten wording

Prompt: “Shorten this note but keep all clinically relevant facts. Professional tone. [your own de-identified draft only]”

Step 5 — Review before charting

Read line by line: accurate? nothing missing? nothing added that didn’t happen? Enter it yourself in your EHR.

Red lines: AI can hallucinate vitals, meds, events. Only you verify what is true. Never let AI write directly into your EHR. Some facilities prohibit AI in charting — check policy first.
