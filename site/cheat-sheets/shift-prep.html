Shift prep — cheat sheet

AI Tools for Nurses • Education only — not medical advice &#bull; Never paste patient data

Before you start

  • Open a free AI tool (see Free AI models cheat sheet).
  • Keep real patient details away — never paste them.
  • Check your facility’s AI policy first.

Step 1 — Pick your focus

Prompt: “I’m an RN working a 12-hour night shift on a med-surg floor. Give me a short shift-prep checklist: common conditions, meds to review, handoff questions. Plain English, education only, no patient data.”

Step 2 — Review conditions

Prompt: “Plain-English refresher on [condition]: what it is, common signs/symptoms, what nurses monitor. 6th-grade level, bullet points, education only.”

Step 3 — Review medications

Prompt: “List 8 common med-surg medications: what each is for, 2–3 common side effects, one thing nurses monitor. No doses, education only.”

Step 4 — Handoff questions

Prompt: “List 8 good handoff questions for med-surg, plus 4 the incoming nurse might ask me. Plain English.”

Step 5 — Practice SBAR

Prompt: “Example SBAR for a nurse calling a provider, made-up patient, no real names/dates. Label: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation.”

Step 6 — Build your checklist

Prompt: “One-page shift-prep checklist for a med-surg night shift: conditions, meds, handoff questions, before-I-leave-home list. Plain English, education only.”

Red lines: Never paste patient names, room numbers, or identifying details. AI can be wrong about doses and facts — verify with your drug reference and clinical judgment. AI does not replace your patient assessment.
