Scheduling — cheat sheet

AI Tools for Nurses • Education only — not medical advice &#bull; Never paste patient data

Step 1 — List your fixed commitments

Write down work shifts, sleep needs, meals, commute, family time, appointments, recharge time.

Step 2 — Build a draft week

Prompt: “I work nights 19:00–07:30 Mon/Wed/Fri, 30-min commute each way. Need 7–8 hrs sleep. Kids’ pickup Tue/Thu 15:00. Grocery Saturday morning. Build one sample week with sleep blocks around nights. Plain English.”

Step 3 — Sleep around night shifts

Prompt: “Simple sleep schedule for three 12-hour night shifts in a row: when to sleep before the first shift, how to flip back to days after. Plain English.”

Step 4 — Time-block your week

Prompt: “Time-block this week: work, sleep, commute, meals, one 20-min walk daily, one restful evening. Low-pressure.”

Step 5 — Adjust for rotation changes

Paste new shift days (no patient/coworker details) and ask AI to re-block the week.

Red lines: No roster, coworker names, or facility scheduling data. AI is not medical advice about sleep — see your clinician for ongoing fatigue. Your manager’s schedule and facility system always win.
