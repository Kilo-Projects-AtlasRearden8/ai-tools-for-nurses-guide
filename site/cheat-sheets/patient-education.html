Patient education — cheat sheet

AI Tools for Nurses • Education only — not medical advice &#bull; Never paste patient data

Ground rules

  • Never paste real patient details into AI.
  • A clinician reviews and approves anything given to a patient.
  • Use your facility’s approved materials first.
  • Use teach-back: ask the patient to explain it back.

Step 1 — Explain a condition

Prompt: “Explain [condition] in plain English for a newly diagnosed patient. 6th-grade level, short sentences, bullet points: what it is, symptoms, why treatment matters. Add: this is general information, ask your care team about your own situation.”

Step 2 — Discharge instructions

Prompt: “Draft plain-language discharge outline after [condition]: medicines, activity, warning signs, follow-up, questions to ask. 6th-grade level. Reminder to call 911 for emergencies.”

Step 3 — Medication teaching

Prompt: “4 short teaching points for [medication]: what it does, how to take it, common side effects, when to call the clinic. Plain English, no doses.”

Step 4 — Simplify instructions

Prompt: “Rewrite these approved facility instructions in plain English, 6th-grade level, short sentences, bullets. Keep all safety warnings. [approved text only, no patient details]”

Step 5 — Teach-back questions

Prompt: “5 friendly teach-back questions to check a patient understands new medication instructions, without sounding like a test.”

Red lines: No patient names, photos, MRNs, or identifying details. AI can invent facts — verify doses and warnings against your drug reference. Give approved materials whenever they exist.
