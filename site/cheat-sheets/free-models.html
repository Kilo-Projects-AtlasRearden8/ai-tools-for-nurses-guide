Free AI models — cheat sheet

AI Tools for Nurses • Education only — not medical advice &#bull; Never paste patient data

A. Browser-based free options (easiest)

  • Major AI providers offer a free tier in a web browser or phone app.
  • Some work without an account.
  • Chats may be stored by the provider — read the privacy policy.

B. Local-first (runs on your device)

  • Ollama (ollama.com) — free, open-source, runs models on your computer.
  • Good if privacy matters most; needs a modern computer.

Step-by-step: browser-based

  1. Pick a provider with a free web tier.
  2. Create an account if required.
  3. Start a new chat and paste an example prompt from this guide.
  4. Read carefully — AI can be wrong.
  5. Delete the chat in settings when done.

Step-by-step: Ollama (local)

  1. Download the free app at ollama.com.
  2. Install and open it.
  3. Choose a small model to start.
  4. Type a prompt — conversation stays on your machine.

Privacy checklist

  • ✓ No patient names, photos, MRNs, or identifying details.
  • ✓ I know the tool may store chats.
  • ✓ My facility allows this AI use.
  • ✓ I verify anything clinical before relying on it.
  • ✓ No passwords, credit cards, or secrets.

Red lines: Free does not mean private. Never paste patient data. AI can be confidently wrong about doses and clinical facts — verify everything.
