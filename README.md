# AI Tools for Nurses & Healthcare Workers

A free beginner manual in plain English: shift prep, patient education, charting help, scheduling, and free AI models. Education only — not medical advice. Never paste patient data.

## What's inside

- [Shift prep](site/shift-prep.html) — conditions, meds, handoff questions, SBAR, your own prep checklist
- [Patient education](site/patient-education.html) — plain-language explanations, discharge drafts, medication teaching, teach-back
- [Charting help](site/charting-help.html) — SOAP/DAP/SOAPIE, turning bullets into notes, tightening wording
- [Scheduling](site/scheduling.html) — personal shift blocks, sleep around nights, time-blocking your week
- [Free AI models](site/free-models.html) — browser-based free options, local-first tools, privacy checklist
- [Printable cheat sheets](site/cheat-sheets/index.html) — one-page sheets to print and keep
- [Safety & privacy](site/safety-policy.html) — education only, never paste patient data

## How to use

Open any `.html` file in `site/` directly in your web browser. No build step, no install, no internet required to read. Cheat sheets are print-optimized (use your browser's Print → Save as PDF).
